01 / The Research

Why Henderson air quality?

Our capstone investigates historical pollutant concentrations measured at Auckland's Henderson monitoring station. We examined seasonal patterns, meteorological relationships and short-term forecasting performance, then presented the findings in a cloud-connected dashboard.

150,432 Hourly observations
2006–23 Historical monitoring period
6 Forecasting methods evaluated

04 / Research Outcomes

What did our models discover?

LightGBM achieved the strongest validation performance for the selected forecasting targets. The following figures show final held-out test R².

0.762 Next-hour NO₂
0.333 Next-hour PM10
0.441 Next 3-hour average PM10
0.387 Next 24-hour average PM10

NO₂ was more predictable than PM10. Abrupt PM10 changes remained challenging, making careful interpretation important.
